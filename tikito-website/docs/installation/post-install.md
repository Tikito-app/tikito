# Post installation

These steps will get you set up after the containers are running.

## Step 1 - Register the admin user

Open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080) and you will see the initial registration screen.

![Initial installation screen](/tikito-screenshots/initial-installation.png)

Fill in a username, email address, and password to create the first (admin) user.

## Step 2 - Create an account

After logging in, create at least one account. An account represents a bank account or broker account.

Go to **Accounts** in the top navigation and click **Add account**. Give it a name (e.g. "ABN AMRO Checking"), an optional account number, and select its currency.

## Step 3 - Import your data

From here you can either:

- [Import banking transactions](/docs/installation/import-money) — for your bank account balances and spending history
- [Import security transactions](/docs/installation/import-security) — for your stock/ETF portfolio

## Step 4 - Explore

Once your data is imported, explore the views:

- **Money** — balance history and transaction groups
- **Securities** — portfolio overview and individual holding graphs
- **Overview** — combined net worth across all accounts
- **Loans** — mortgage or student loan tracking
