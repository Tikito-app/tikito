# Import your banking transactions
After creating an account for your banking transactions, you can now use the export of your bank and import it into Tikito.
Several [files](/docs/importing/money) are supported. Please refer to you bank on details how to export your transactions.

## Step 1 - Import the file from your bank
Click on `Import` from the top menu. Select the account you created for your money transactions and the file you exported from your bank.

## Step 2 - Create money groups
In order to group transactions together, you can create groups by clicking on `Money groups` in the top menu.

## Step 3 - Apply the grouping
From the top menu to go `Admin` and click in the left menu on `Money` and you will see a list of securities.

## Visualize it
Now you can go to `Money` from the top menu and you will see the graph view.
