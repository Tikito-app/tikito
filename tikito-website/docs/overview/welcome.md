# Welcome to Tikito

Tikito is a **self-hosted personal finance tracker**. It lets you import your bank and broker transactions, track stocks, ETFs, and cryptocurrencies, visualise how your portfolio has grown over time, and manage loans — all without sharing your financial data with any third party.

## Why Tikito?

Most portfolio trackers either cost money or require you to hand over access to your financial accounts. Tikito runs entirely on your own server (or laptop) using Docker. Your data stays with you.

## What you can track

- **Bank accounts** — import exports from ABN AMRO, ING, Bunq, and others. See your balance history over time and categorise spending with transaction groups.
- **Investment portfolio** — import stock and ETF transactions from DeGiro (or any broker via a generic CSV). Track your performance, dividends, and cost basis.
- **Cryptocurrency** — holdings on Bitvavo or through a broker are both supported.
- **Loans** — model mortgages (annuity and linear) and other loans. Compare scheduled payments with your actual bank transactions.

Follow the [Quick start](/docs/overview/quick-start) guide to get Tikito running in minutes using Docker Compose.
