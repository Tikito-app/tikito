# Welcome to Tikito

My name is Nick and I have been looking for a place where I can get insights into my assets. 
However, I had to either pay with money to get these insights, or pay with my personal data. 
That is why I created Tikito, so that anyone can get free insights into their assets.

We are still in a startup phase, meaning not all things might work and a lot of features still need to be added. 

Feel free to try it, look around or even contribute!
